Questions

Before you book.

Can I have a gown made for me?

Yes. Custom gowns are designed around you, with fittings along the way. Message us your date and ideas to start.

Do you hire out gowns?

Yes. Send the date and the style you like and we reply with what is available.

Can you match a veil to my gown?

Yes. Veils are made to sit with the gown so the whole look matches.

How do I get a price?

Prices depend on the gown, fabric and beading. Share your idea on WhatsApp and you get a quote for your choice.
